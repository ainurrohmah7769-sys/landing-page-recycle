PAPER WASTE OBSERVATION



Waste is the residue or discarded material from an activity or production process, whether industrial or household, that no longer has any economic or marketable value. Many types of waste can be recycled, especially since Indonesia is the second-largest producer of waste in the world. Indonesia produces a wide variety of waste, including plastic, bottles, cardboard, paper, and more. Based on my observations, I will discuss and explain paper waste.


Paper is a material made from discarded wood fibers. While biodegradable, large quantities can be a burden on the environment and produce greenhouse gases. As students, we undoubtedly have a large amount of unused books. We can donate books that are still usable to the less fortunate, and we can recycle the ones that are no longer usable into valuable items.


We can find various kinds of paper, such as HVS paper, folio, cardboard and others. Each paper must also have a variety of colors, for example the gray color of paper in worksheet books and test paper, the common white color of paper, and various colors of cardboard. Paper also has various thicknesses and textures, but most paper has a smooth and thin texture.


Recycling paper waste offers numerous benefits, including increased revenue through the sale of recycled products, conserving natural resources like trees and oil, and ultimately reducing the amount of waste in Indonesia. To reduce this paper waste, I created a paper clock. My goal is to remind people that when they look at a clock, they're not only remembering the time but also reminding them that we must recycle and reduce waste for a sustainable environment.

PAPER WASTE OBSERVATION



​Waste is residual or discarded material that can accumulate and cause negative impacts if not properly managed. Many types of waste can be utilized, one of which is paper waste. Based on the results of my observation this time, I will explain what we can utilize from paper waste.


Paper is a thin sheet made from fibers, such as wood fiber, grass fiber, and cloth fiber. Paper functions as a medium for writing, drawing, and printing. However, in its use, many sheets of paper are often discarded unnecessarily due to overproduction, leading to a large accumulation of paper waste.


​There are various kinds of paper produced, such as HVS paper (writing paper), cardboard, fancy paper, duplex board, and others. HVS paper is generally used for writing and printing, cardboard is commonly used to make packaging, fancy paper is used for invitations because of its unique texture and color, and duplex board is used for thick advertising cards. Besides those I mentioned, many other types of paper are produced worldwide with different materials and different uses.


The benefits of paper waste include its potential to be used to make various creations, such as: storage containers, photo frames, paper bags, flower vases, and others. Additionally, unused scraps of paper and books can be collected and turned into new paper for writing, which helps save money so there is no need to buy new paper. From all this, we can also sell them to interested people, which can be a modest source of extra income.

PAPER WASTE OBSERVATION



Waste is any solid waste that is no longer used and discarded. Its management is crucial to preventing environmental problems and involves decomposition, sorting, collection, management, and final processing. Furthermore, waste is considered to have no use value or utility for its owner and is ultimately discarded into the environment.


Paper waste is a common type of waste found in our environment, especially in offices, schools, and households. Visually, the waste comes in various forms, ranging from small pieces to large sheets, with a texture that is generally rough and easily torn. The color of paper waste varies, depending on the type of paper and ink used, ranging from white, brown, and others. The smell of paper waste is usually not too strong, unless it is mixed with organic waste or exposed to water. The presence of unmanaged paper waste can have very bad potential, such as the spread of diseases that cause a lot of problems.


Therefore, proper paper waste management, such as recycling, is crucial for reducing negative environmental impacts. Paper offers numerous benefits to human life, including its use as a stationery or printing medium, documentation, and reporting, as well as for art and creativity. Paper plays a vital role in various sectors, from education to industry. Wise paper use and proper paper waste management are crucial for environmental sustainability. Paper recycling is a solution to reduce tree felling and conserve energy.


Paper offers numerous benefits to the environment, including energy savings, reduced environmental pollution, reduced waste, reduced paper use, and its use as fertilizer. Awareness of the importance of environmentally friendly paper management is growing, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and saving trees.
